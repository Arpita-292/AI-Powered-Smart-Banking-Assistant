What does this doughnut show?
Shows the verification status breakdown of all customer Aadhaar identity submissions.
  • ●
    Green (Approved): Fully verified accounts with valid Aadhaar & live selfie match.
  • ●
    Amber (Pending): Waiting for admin review in the KYC Verification tab.
  • ●
    Red (Rejected): Submissions with mismatched data or rejected selfie.
How to use: Ensure pending queue stays near 0 by approving verified customers.

Bank Reserves & Loans
What does this balance bar show?
Visualizes bank solvency, liquidity reserves, and active lending exposure.
  • ●
    Bank Reserves: Liquid cash reserves held safely in the bank vault.
  • ●
    Customer Deposits: Total savings deposited by all account holders.
  • ●
    Loans Extended: Total active credit lines disbursed to customers.
How to use: Keep bank reserves higher than loans to maintain 100% financial health.
